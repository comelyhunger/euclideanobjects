Privacy Policy

Euclidean Objects ("Euclidean Objects," "we," "us") is a sole-proprietor creative studio owned by Fable Bartin in Millbrook, New York. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect through the website at euclideanobjects.com and through our text-message (SMS) program, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect on the website

The site is static. It does not use accounts, cookies set by us, tracking pixels, or advertising. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under Google's privacy policy. If you email us, we keep the email.

2. Information we collect through the SMS program

When you opt in to text messages, we collect your mobile phone number, the date and method of your opt-in, and your opt-in or opt-out status. We keep the content of messages sent and received on the line, as any text conversation is kept on a phone.

3. How we use it

To send you the messages you asked for (reminders, task and status updates, scheduling confirmations, and replies to your questions), and to honor STOP and HELP requests. We do not use your number for marketing.

4. Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

The only party that processes your messages is our messaging service provider (Twilio), which delivers them on our behalf under its own privacy policy. We do not sell, rent, or trade phone numbers or opt-in information.

5. Retention

Opt-out records are kept indefinitely so that we do not message you again. Other program records are kept while the conversation is active and deleted on request.

6. Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of texts. Email fable@prodigalparadigm.com to ask what we hold about your number or to have it deleted.

7. Children

The site and the SMS program are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.

9. Contact

Euclidean Objects, Fable Bartin, sole proprietor, Millbrook, NY 12545. Email fable@prodigalparadigm.com.
