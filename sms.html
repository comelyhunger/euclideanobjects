Text messages from Euclidean Objects

Euclidean Objects is a sole-proprietor creative studio owned by Fable Bartin in Millbrook, New York: art, writing, and research, built in conversation. Euclidean Objects operates a text-message line for short conversational messages with people who have asked to receive them. This page is the opt-in call to action and the full program terms.

To opt in, text START or YES to:

+1 (251) 322-7846

By texting START or YES to this number, or by texting the number first, you consent to receive conversational text messages from Euclidean Objects: reminders, task and status updates, scheduling confirmations, and replies to questions you have texted in. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What you will receive

  • Reminders about meetings, calls, and deadlines you have asked about
  • Task and status updates on work in progress
  • Scheduling confirmations (reply YES to confirm, NO to reschedule)
  • Short replies to questions you have sent by text

No marketing. No promotions. No bulk sending. Messages go only to numbers that have opted in as described above, never to purchased lists or numbers collected from third parties.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with the conversation and is typically a few messages per week at most. Msg & data rates may apply according to your carrier plan. Euclidean Objects does not charge for messages.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. Text START to re-subscribe.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or email fable@prodigalparadigm.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Supported on major US carriers; availability may vary.
